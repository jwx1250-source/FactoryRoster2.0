Section 7:
FAQ

Questions:
1. Are all factories on FactoryRoster verified?
Answer:
Yes. Only factory profiles that have passed FactoryRoster’s required verification checks are published in search results.

2. Does FactoryRoster participate in transactions?
Answer:
No. FactoryRoster provides verified factory information and contact intelligence only. Buyers and factories manage their own transactions.

3. What does Business Contact Verified mean?
Answer:
It means FactoryRoster has manually checked the business contact information before the profile was listed.

4. What does Factory Evidence mean?
Answer:
It means photos or video evidence of the factory or facility were reviewed before publication.

5. Does FactoryRoster guarantee supplier quality?
Answer:
No. Verification confirms identity, contactability, and factory evidence. Buyers should still conduct product, compliance, sample, and contract due diligence.

6. Can I request updated verification?
Answer:
Yes. Buyers can request additional verification support such as updated contact checks, photos, or video review.
